# What's new in LGA Mighty Tools

## v1.53 (2026-11-09)

- **New:** In General, Colors lets you choose one of six color palettes: Violet, High contrast, Graphite | amber, Mist, Warm teal and Magenta; they apply at once and, with the menu open, the arrow keys preview them before you choose.
- **New:** The update window shows what's new in the new version, in the language you chose for Mighty Tools.
- **New:** Escape closes the Mighty Tools window and leaves it in the tray (on Mac, in the menu bar), and Ctrl+Alt with the left and right arrows (⌘⌥ on Mac) goes to the previous or next tool in the list.
- **New:** Meet Frame: Alt+Shift+Win+M (on Mac, ⌘⌥⇧M) shows a frame to share only that part of the screen in Teams, Meet or Zoom; notifications, the desktop, WhatsApp, Telegram and the apps you choose are hidden from the others, Pause freezes the image, and the frame stretches to any size or, with «Lock the frame shape», keeps the 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or current shape.
- **New:** Dictation types what you say into any text field: press Win+H (or hold it while you speak), see the text in a small window as you talk, and it is typed when you finish without touching what you had copied or leaving a trace in the Win+V history; it writes VFX and development terms correctly (Nuke Studio, compo, DMP, commit, push) and you can add your own in «Custom words»; it runs on your computer and the model is downloaded only once with «Download» from its page. (Windows only)
- **New:** Disk Space's clean-up finds the caches of Nuke, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, Premiere, Ableton, Topaz Video, REAPER, Unreal and other video and audio programs even if you moved them to another drive, and leaves for you to decide what is costly to rebuild, such as proxies, optimized media or models.
- **New:** Disk Space's clean-up finds the installer copies Windows keeps for programs that are no longer installed and sends them to the Recycle Bin with administrator permission; those of installed programs never appear. (Windows only)
- **New:** Mouse Buttons makes a mouse button hold keys and the left button only in the apps you choose, and includes, turned off, an example rule that pans the image in Photoshop with the middle button. (Windows only)
- **New:** App Toggle opens an app with a shortcut, brings it to the front if it is already open or minimizes it if you are already using it (on Mac it hides it), and includes, turned off, OBS as an example and, on Windows, Win+E for XYplorer or, if you don't have it, for File Explorer without opening a new window every time.
- **New:** Desktop Icons saves where the desktop icons are, once a day or whenever you want with a name, and puts them back with one click when Windows scrambles them, with «Undo» to go back. (Windows only)
- **New:** Window Layouts can now also move Task Manager and apps running as administrator: turn on «Also move administrator windows» at the top of its panel. (Windows only)
- **Improved:** Mighty Tools is lighter and faster: the window opens and switches pages instantly, and closed in the tray it uses less than half the memory, without losing anything you were editing.
- **Improved:** If the connection fails, checking for updates, downloading a new version and downloading the Dictation model retry on their own, every five seconds and up to three times per server, and show «Retrying (2 of 4)…» while they wait; an interrupted download resumes where it left off.
- **Improved:** Mighty Tools has a new design: each tool is a sheet on a black background joined to its place in the list on the left, and the colors mean something: cyan is what is running, violet what you can click and yellow what needs attention; the text is as large as before.
- **Improved:** Tools appear in alphabetical order in the window, in the tray menu and in General.
- **Improved:** The «?» no longer opens a help page for all the tools together: each page has its own «How it works» with that tool's steps, closed when you open the page, which opens with a click or with the «?»; the version, the author and the website are at the bottom of General.
- **Improved:** In General, the interface size is now called Zoom and shows 100 %, 110 % and 120 %. (Windows only)
- **Improved:** Releasing the keys of a Window Layouts or App Toggle shortcut no longer opens the Start menu or the Office app (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Win, the combination sent by Stream Deck and other programmable keyboards), and Win plus a letter (like Win+E) can be recorded to replace the Windows shortcut. (Windows only)
- **Improved:** The window is a little taller and no page cuts off text or buttons: if something doesn't fit, the scroll bar appears.
- **Improved:** The scroll bar is thinner.
- **Improved:** All on and off options use the same switch instead of checkboxes, and the switch slides smoothly when you click it.
- **Fixed:** The Mighty Tools window has rounded corners like the system's and moves by dragging from any free part of the top bar, not just some. (macOS only)
- **Fixed:** Link Redirector no longer alters the links it forwards to the browser: sign-ins with Google and other services that ended in «Error 400: invalid_request» work again. (macOS only)
- **Fixed:** Window Layouts no longer stays red with «Needs attention» when a shortcut is pressed with no window to move: it says in yellow what was in front (the desktop, a minimized window…) and when, and the note clears with «Dismiss» or with the next shortcut that works.
- **Fixed:** Open in NukeX's Apply no longer closes the app when Windows has to ask which program should open .nk files. (Windows only)

## v1.52 (2026-10-14)

- **Improved:** Disk Space's Clean up shows how much each group and row will actually free next to its total, and how many of its folders are held by open apps.
- **Improved:** Disk Space's What changed groups the folders that grew by area, lists each one only once so the numbers add up, and shows how the growth splits between areas.
- **Fixed:** Window Layouts shortcuts sent by Stream Deck apply the layout instead of reaching Windows or other apps. (Windows only)

## v1.47

- **Fixed:** Scrolling over the reminder time in Disk Space no longer changes your choice; choose a time from the menu to change it.

## v1.43

- **New:** Window Layouts lets you add window shortcuts to move or minimize the active window: combine horizontal and vertical halves, thirds or quarters, draw an area, or copy an open window's bounds while keeping clear of visible system bars.
- **New:** Window Layouts tries to take priority for active shortcuts, marks detected clashes with a yellow dot inside its panel, and lets you turn each layout on or off separately. (Windows only)

## v1.37

- **Improved:** Disk Space reminders let you edit the minimum free space next to the reminder time, using the drive's GB or percentage setting, in a smaller alert with aligned buttons and a clearer "Remind me again" action.

## v1.33 (2026-10-04)

- **Fixed:** Checking for updates works: version 1.31 could not connect, so download this version once from the download page. (macOS only)

## v1.31 (2026-10-04)

- **Improved:** The app waits to offer or install an update while Disk Space is deleting files.

## v1.30

- **New:** LGA Mighty Tools is now available for macOS, on Apple Silicon and Intel, with its five tools: Open in NukeX, Nuke Shortcuts, Disk Space, Folder Switch and Link Redirector. (macOS only)
- **New:** The app checks for new versions and installs them for you in one click. (macOS only)
- **Fixed:** Updating from inside the app works now: earlier versions stop with an integrity error, so install this one once with the installer from the download page. (Windows only)

## v1.27

- **Improved:** The app checks for a new version every few hours while it stays open, not only at startup, and Later postpones the notice for a day.
